====================================================
  NV-2.0 — PRIVACY POLICY
====================================================

Effective date: 2026-08-15

NV-2.0 is a single-player sandbox/survival game. It collects the
minimum possible data and shares nothing with third parties except
where described below.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT
   - Nothing that identifies you personally is required to play.
   - Local files only: your world saves, settings and AI checkpoints
     are stored on your own device in your user data directory
     (Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\NV2; Linux: ~/.local/share/nv2).
     These never leave your machine.

2. NETWORK REQUESTS
   - Optional: when you start a new world and your machine is online,
     NV-2.0 queries the NASA POWER climate API
     (power.larc.nasa.gov) to fetch real climate data for the
     coordinates derived from your world seed. This request sends the
     coordinates and a fixed date range only — no personal data, no
     account, no device fingerprint. If the request fails, the game
     falls back to synthetic offline climate; the game is fully
     playable without network access.
   - No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising SDKs.

3. THIRD PARTIES
   - NASA POWER: data used under NASA's data policy
     (https://power.larc.nasa.gov). NV-2.0 is not affiliated with NASA.

4. DRM / LICENSE KEYS
   - Purchased copies are tied to a license key issued at sale. Keys
     are verified locally (HMAC digest); no key material or purchase
     data is transmitted.

5. CHILDREN
   - The game contains no chat, no user-generated content sharing, no
     microtransactions and no external links. It is suitable for all
     ages (PEGI 3 / ESRB E).

6. CONTACT
   - For any privacy question: <support@nv2-0.example> (replace with
     your real support address before launch).

7. CHANGES
   - This policy may be updated; the version published on the store
     page at the time of purchase applies.
